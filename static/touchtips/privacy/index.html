TouchTips privacy

Effective September 6, 2026

TouchTips is developed by Harivansh Rathi. It stores meeting details and notes on your iPhone. You do not need a TouchTips account.

Contacts, location, and notes

With your permission, TouchTips reads your contacts to show people and notice new additions. It uses available location information to suggest where you may have met. You can correct meeting details and add notes in the app.

TouchTips does not send your contacts, meeting history, or notes to a server operated by the developer. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Apple services

TouchTips uses Apple's Contacts, location, mapping, and place-search services. Maps and place searches may communicate with Apple. Apple's services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Device backups and contact syncing are controlled by your iPhone and account settings, separately from TouchTips.

Your choices

You can change Contacts, Location, and Notification permissions in iPhone Settings. In TouchTips, you can edit or remove a person's meeting details and note. “Delete all data” in TouchTips settings clears its records without deleting your iPhone contacts.

Support

If you email for help, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to send, and uses it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending private contact details.

For privacy questions, email rathiharivansh@gmail.com.
