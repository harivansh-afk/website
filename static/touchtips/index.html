Remember everyone.
Forget no one.

TouchTips keeps meeting details alongside your iPhone contacts. Remember when and where you met someone, add a note, and find them again by date or place.

Browse your people in a list, follow your timeline, or find them on a map. Your meeting details and notes stay on your iPhone. No account required.

Get help

Email rathiharivansh@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened. Please leave private contact details out of screenshots.

Finding your people

Grant Contacts access during onboarding. You can change access later in iPhone Settings. Switch between the People list and timeline in TouchTips settings, or open the map to browse by meeting place.

Missing or incorrect details

Open a person to edit the date, place, or note. TouchTips suggests details when it notices new contacts, but iOS does not notify a suspended app about every contact change. Return to TouchTips after adding a contact, then review the suggestion.

Location permission helps with place suggestions. Notifications are optional.

Removing meeting details

Swipe left on a person and confirm to remove their TouchTips meeting details and note. This keeps the person in your iPhone contacts. To clear TouchTips records, open the app's settings and choose “Delete all data.”
